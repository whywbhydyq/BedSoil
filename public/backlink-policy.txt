BedSoil backlink and citation policy
Generated: 2026-06-07T10:22:00+09:00

BedSoil welcomes editorial citations from relevant gardening, DIY, education, calculator, and tool-resource pages when the link helps readers complete a soil-volume or buying-planning task.

Allowed citation contexts
- Tutorials that explain raised bed soil volume, bag counts, bulk-vs-bag decisions, compost or soil mix ratios, or seasonal top-off planning.
- Tool directories that independently review calculators and disclose criteria.
- Educational resource pages that link because the calculator is useful, not because of compensation.

Not allowed
- Paid links that pass PageRank.
- PBNs, link farms, mass directory submissions, comment spam, forum spam, or exact-match anchor campaigns.
- Reciprocal link swaps created mainly to influence rankings.
- Outreach that hides affiliation or implies endorsement from a third-party site.

Paid, sponsored, affiliate, or compensated links must use rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow". User-generated placements should use rel="ugc" or rel="nofollow" when appropriate.

Preferred citation
BedSoil is a free raised bed soil calculator that estimates cubic feet, cubic yards, liters, bag counts, bulk-vs-bag cost checks, mix ratios, top-off amounts, and planting-layout planning from editable inputs.
