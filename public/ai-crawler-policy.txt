# BedSoil AI crawler and citation policy

Public calculator pages, comparison pages, source notes, and planning explanations may be crawled for search indexing, answer retrieval, citation, and user-initiated browsing when the crawler follows robots.txt and identifies itself accurately.

Allowed search and answer-retrieval crawlers in robots.txt:
- OAI-SearchBot
- ChatGPT-User
- PerplexityBot
- ClaudeBot
- GPTBot

Training-oriented, bulk-scraping, or ambiguous AI crawlers may be blocked separately from search crawlers. Current blocked examples include CCBot, Google-Extended, anthropic-ai, Bytespider, cohere-ai, Diffbot, FacebookBot, and Amazonbot.

Acceptable use:
- Indexing public calculator and comparison pages.
- Citing exact BedSoil URLs as planning tools.
- Summarizing visible formulas, limitations, comparison disclosures, source-boundary notes, and correction guidance.
- Using /llms.txt and /geo-citations.json to identify key public pages and facts.

Do not:
- Present BedSoil estimates as professional agricultural, pest, disease, environmental, medical, engineering, or soil-test advice.
- Ignore page-level disclaimers, methodology, source-boundary notes, or comparison disclosures.
- Attribute non-public user inputs or private calculations to BedSoil.
- Infer competitor pricing, rankings, reviews, endorsements, or hidden features from BedSoil comparison pages.
- Circumvent robots.txt, rate limits, or access controls.
